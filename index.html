My Research Interests

Data-driven weather models beat operational NWP on aggregate skill scores but stay inexplicable to the forecasters who act on them, and they fail most on localized extreme events. Is that from L2 training, GNNs' spectral bias, or training on smoothed reanalysis data?

I think the loss function is the place to start. An L2/MSE objective is minimized by the conditional mean E[Y|X], the average over all plausible futures, so it averages out the rare, sharp features a forecaster cares about. More data or compute does not fix this; a trained MSE model is the blur. I'm working on testing two ways out of this: model the full distribution and sample a future (diffusion models like GenCast), or predict a learned representation instead of the field (JEPA).
